## Customer Success Plan

**Example-authored reusable plan.** The milestones below are proposed activities, not completed customer outcomes or a promised NI engagement.

### Define Success With the Customer

Agree one desired outcome, the baseline evidence available today, and the condition that will justify continuing. Name the engineering owner, business owner, and person responsible for retaining evidence.

| Milestone | Deliverable | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish context | System boundary, upcoming change, constraints | Is the problem bounded enough to evaluate? |
| Inspect architecture | Change walkthrough and responsibility map | Are the proposed boundaries testable? |
| Trial verification | Repeatable check and recorded environment | Is the evidence reliable? |
| Rehearse delivery | Traceable build and recovery procedure | Can the team repeat and recover the release? |
| Review adoption | Maintainer handoff and unresolved risks | Continue, revise, or stop? |

### Review Without Manufacturing Progress

Record what happened, the evidence inspected, what remains uncertain, and the next owner. An incomplete experiment is not a failed customer; it is information for the next decision. Do not substitute a dashboard status for a reviewed artifact.

### Sustain the Practice

Schedule a review of dependency support, retained build environments, team knowledge, and unresolved exceptions. Keep a named maintainer and an explicit path for onboarding a new engineer.
