## Share the Delivery Without Losing the Boundaries

**Example-authored partner guidance.** This page does not endorse a partner or state an official NI partner service scope.

### Agree Responsibilities

Document ownership of architecture decisions, application code, drivers, test fixtures, build environments, licensing, deployment, and support. Record how an unresolved issue is escalated and who accepts a deliverable.

### Make the Handoff Reproducible

Retain the supported environment, dependency inventory, source revision, build steps, acceptance evidence, and known limitations. Verify the recipient can access every required private repository and external artifact; a link alone is not proof of access.

### Choose an Actual Confidentiality Boundary

Everyone with repository read access can inspect its contents and history. If NI-only and partner-shareable material require different access, use separate approved repositories or another approved boundary. Do not rely on draft status, hidden navigation, or audience tags to protect content.

This personal-account reference is an explicitly approved public website backed by a private repository. Invited collaborators receive write access. It does not establish a production read-only partner model.

### Validate Together

Use the same change scenario and acceptance conditions across the customer and partner teams. Ask the receiving team to reproduce a build and explain a failure path before concluding the transfer is complete.
